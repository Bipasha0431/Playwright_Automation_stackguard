# Cart Test Plan

## Feature summary

The Swag Labs cart page (/cart.html) lists the items added from the product list with quantity, name, description, price and a Remove button, and offers Continue Shopping and Checkout buttons. This plan covers the cart page contents, removing items on the cart page, the empty cart, Continue Shopping, the cart icon, persistence, the Checkout button navigating to checkout step one, and two edge cases. Adding from the product list, sorting, detail pages, login, checkout steps and logout are out of scope and covered by other plans.

## Application URL / environment

- URL under test: https://www.saucedemo.com/cart.html (public production demo)
- Every scenario starts in a fresh browser context, signed in on the product list with an empty cart, unless the row says otherwise.

## Open questions / notes

- The empty cart shows no "empty" message or call to action (CART-005, CART-006); confirm that is intended. The Checkout button is still enabled on an empty cart (not followed up here; checkout plan).
- Quantity is always 1 and cannot be changed on the cart page; there is no quantity control (observed).
